# Workforce Management Guidelines

Workforce analytics can be used to identify employees who may
require additional support.

## Relevant indicators may include:

- Attrition risk
- Skill-gap risk
- Performance
- Job level
- Career progression
- Compensation indicators
- Workload
- Employee experience

Machine-learning predictions should be treated as decision-support
signals rather than definitive conclusions.

Managers and HR teams should consider additional employee context
before taking action.
