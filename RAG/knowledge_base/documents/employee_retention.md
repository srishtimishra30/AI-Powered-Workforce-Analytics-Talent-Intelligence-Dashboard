# Employee Retention Guidelines

## Purpose:
These guidelines provide general recommendations for identifying
and addressing potential employee retention risks.

## Retention Risk Indicators:

1. High workload
Employees consistently working excessive hours may experience
higher levels of stress and reduced job satisfaction.

2. Career stagnation
Employees who have remained in the same role for an extended
period without career progression may require career-development
discussions.

3. Low job satisfaction
Low employee satisfaction can be an indicator of potential
retention concerns.

4. Performance concerns
Employees experiencing performance difficulties may benefit from
additional support, training, and manager feedback.

## Recommended Actions:

- Review employee workload.
- Discuss career-development opportunities.
- Identify training requirements.
- Review employee engagement.
- Provide appropriate manager support.
