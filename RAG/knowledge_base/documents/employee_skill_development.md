# Employee Skill Development Guidelines

## Purpose:
These guidelines describe general approaches for identifying
and addressing employee skill-development needs.

## Skill Development Indicators:

1. Low performance
Employees with consistently low performance may require
additional training or mentoring.

2. Lack of career progression
Employees without recent career progression may benefit from
career-development planning.

3. Role mismatch
Employees whose current capabilities do not align with their
role requirements may require targeted skill development.

4. Training requirements
Employees identified as having skill gaps should be evaluated
for appropriate learning and development opportunities.

## Recommended Actions:

- Identify the employee's missing competencies.
- Compare current capabilities with role requirements.
- Recommend targeted training.
- Provide mentoring or coaching.
- Review progress periodically.
